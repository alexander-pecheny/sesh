Sesh

An SSH and mosh terminal for iPhone and iPad.

Sesh reaches your own machines over SSH or mosh. Nothing runs locally: every terminal is a window onto a remote shell, drawn by libghostty, so colours, ligatures and escape sequences behave as they do on a desktop. A mosh session survives a lost network and a locked phone.

Keys and passwords live in the iOS Keychain. Hosts stay on the device. Touch modes let you work a TUI by finger, and the two rows of keys carry the modifiers a terminal needs.
