Source and licences

Sesh is free software.

A build links mosh, through rmosh, so the app as a whole is covered by the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later, and comes with no warranty. Sesh's own source is MIT. Anyone holding a build is entitled to its corresponding source, which is why both repositories are public and clone anonymously:

  • The app: code.pecheny.me/pecheny/sesh
  • Mosh, ported to Rust: code.pecheny.me/pecheny/rmosh

Mosh's copyright holders waived the clash between the GPL and Apple's terms in COPYING.iOS, which is what lets a GPL build ship through the App Store at all. The binary carries libghostty, rmosh, 141 Rust crates, the font, the icons and the palettes. Every one of their licence texts is in the app under Settings, Licences, and in docs/LICENSES.md in the repository.

Mosh is a registered trademark of its authors. Sesh links code derived from mosh rather than reimplementing the protocol.
