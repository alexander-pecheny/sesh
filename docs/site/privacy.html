Sesh Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Sesh collects no data.

Sesh connects to the machines you name and to nothing else. There is no server the developer runs, no account to create, and no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind in the app.

What stays on your device

Your Hosts, the addresses and usernames you type, the known-host fingerprints Sesh records on first connection, and the Drafts you write in the Editor are stored in the app's own container on the device. Private keys and any password you ask Sesh to save are held in the iOS Keychain. None of it is transmitted anywhere, and the developer has no way to read it.

What leaves your device

Only what a terminal session is: the keystrokes you send to a machine you chose, and the output it sends back, over SSH or mosh, encrypted end to end between the app and that machine. Sesh does not relay traffic through anything in between. What the remote machine logs is governed by whoever runs it — which, for most users of a terminal, is you.

If a program running on the remote machine sets the clipboard through OSC 52, Sesh writes to the phone's clipboard. Clipboard reads from the remote machine are refused.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything Sesh holds, including its Keychain items. There is no account to close and no deletion request to file.

Children

Sesh is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, regardless of age.
